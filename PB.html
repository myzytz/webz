Siquijor Trip

"I'm thrilled to share my second adventure to the enchanting island of Siquijor, a trip that was all about carefree exploration and reconnecting with friends. At the invitation of some dear friends who call Siquijor home, I embarked on a journey to discover the island's hidden gems. We opted for a relaxed approach, renting a cozy room at a local resort and spending our days meandering around the island, soaking up its natural beauty and warm atmosphere. Every moment was a treasure, and I'm already counting down the days until my next visit. Siquijor, I'll be back soon!"

Baslay Hotspring

"I'd always dreamed of visiting the Baslay Dauin Hotspring, even after a near miss during a previous trip. The thought of experiencing it lingered until, with a sudden burst of perfect weather, enough energy, and a little extra cash, I decided to just go for it – no elaborate plans needed! And what an experience it was. The hotsprings offered a stunning overlook and a series of tiered stairs that reminded me so much of the beautiful Cambugahay Falls in Siquijor, only here, the stairs were even longer, leading to a delightful difference: wonderfully hot water! It truly was a nice and spontaneous journey."
